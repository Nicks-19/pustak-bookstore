1. Motivation & Context

Problem Statement: Campus Retail Inefficiencies

Traditional college bookstores operate as static point-of-sale cash registers that ignore the primary economic and logistical pain points of students.

💸

Textbook Depreciation

Students spend ₹3,000–₹5,000 every semester on reference books that sit unused after 4 months. No transparent circular buyback system exists to recycle them.

⏱️

Floor Search Latency

Walk-in students and cashiers spend 10–15 minutes wandering physical aisles searching for books without spatial coordinate guidance.

📉

Locked Dead Stock

25%–35% of retailer working capital is frozen in slow-moving titles with zero predictive markdown or liquidation automation.

3. Innovation Matrix

Five High-Scoring Differentiating Features

1. 🔄 Circular Buyback Engine

Grades condition (Mint 50%, Good 40%, Fair 25%, Poor 10%). Credits student store wallet and automatically injects pre-owned copies on Rack P.

2. 🗺️ Physical Store Shelf Navigator

4D coordinates (Aisle ➔ Section ➔ Rack ➔ Shelf) visualized on an interactive 2D canvas, finding books in under 5 seconds.

3. 📦 One-Click Syllabus Bundler

Bundles mandatory semester curriculum textbooks with an automated 15% discount and single-click checkout.

4. 📉 Dead Stock AI & Clearance

Identifies dormant stock (>60 or >90 days without sale), computes locked working capital, and triggers 1-click clearance discounts.

5. 🧾 Fast POS Checkout & Dynamic Digital Invoicing

Atomic inventory decrement, student store wallet credit redemption, and paperless digital receipts with verification QR codes.

4. Database Architecture

Normalized Relational Schema (3NF)

7 relational tables with foreign keys and integrity constraints:

1. books
isbn (UQ), title, author, mrp, selling_price, stock_quantity, aisle_no, section, rack_id, shelf_level, last_sold_at
2. student_wallets
student_prn (PK), student_name, contact, wallet_balance_inr
3. buyback_transactions
id, student_prn (FK), book_title, condition_grade, buyback_amount_inr
4. syllabus_bundles
id, bundle_name, course_branch, semester, discount_percent
5. bundle_items
id, bundle_id (FK), book_id (FK)
6. orders
invoice_no (UQ), customer_prn, subtotal_inr, wallet_redeemed_inr, total_paid_inr
7. order_items
id, order_id (FK), book_id (FK), title, unit_price, quantity, total_price
